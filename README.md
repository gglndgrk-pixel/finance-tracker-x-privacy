# Finance Tracker X: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 20 October 2026*

Finance Tracker X is a personal finance app written for, and used by, its owner and the people they share it with.

## What data the app handles

You type in, or import, your own account balances, tax-year figures, to-dos and bank transactions. The app stores them on your own device, in a vault folder that you choose.

## Where your data is kept

- **Only on your devices.** The vault is encrypted with your passphrase (Argon2id key derivation, AES-256-GCM). Without the passphrase the files can't be read.
- **No servers.** The app has no back end and no account. It doesn't make network connections.
- **Syncing is yours.** If you put the vault folder in a folder that Syncthing (or another tool you run) shares between your own devices, that tool copies the encrypted files. The app never sends them anywhere itself.

## What we collect

Nothing. There is no telemetry, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind. The developer receives no data from the app.

## Microsoft Store

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download and updates under its own privacy statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com). The developer may see the aggregate install and crash statistics that Partner Center shows to every publisher. These contain none of your financial data.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the Store version removes its settings. Your vault stays in the folder you chose until you delete it. Delete that folder, on every device that syncs it, to remove your data completely.

## Children

The app isn't aimed at children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page, with a new date at the top. Questions: open an issue on this repository, or contact the publisher shown on the Store listing.
